# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Name:** Personal Planning App (AI MasterClass Course Project)
**Goal:** A full-stack personal planning application to manage daily/weekly tasks, time blocking, and reminders.

## Target Audience
- Single personal user.

## Core Features
1. **Task & Schedule Management:**
   - Create, edit, and delete tasks and events.
   - Time blocking and reminders.
2. **AI Agentic Workflows (Combined):**
   - **AI Schedule Conflict Resolver:** Automatically detects overlaps in the schedule and proposes rearranged time slots.
   - **AI Daily Briefing & Reflection:** Generates a morning summary of the day's goals and an evening reflection on what was accomplished.
   - **AI Habit Coach:** Recommends optimal times to build habits and tracks consistency.

## Interfaces / Components
1. **Server (Backend):** Handles business logic, AI agent coordination, and data persistence.
2. **Web Frontend:** A rich browser interface for planning out the week and managing complex tasks.
3. **Mobile App:** A streamlined interface for checking the schedule on the go and ticking off tasks.
4. **CLI (Command Line Interface):** A terminal tool primarily used for quick task entry and viewing the daily schedule.
